The third beta of BackDown 1.0: folders open in a moment on very large indexes, and the app shows when it's waiting.

## Install

| Platform | Installer | Command line |
|---|---|---|
| Linux x86-64 | `backdown-io_1.0.0-beta.3_amd64.deb` (Debian, Ubuntu) or `.AppImage` | `backdown-v1.0.0-beta.3-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` (static) |
| Linux ARM64 | `backdown-io_1.0.0-beta.3_arm64.deb` or `.AppImage` | `backdown-v1.0.0-beta.3-linux-arm64.tar.gz` (static) |
| macOS (Apple silicon + Intel) | `BackDown-1.0.0-beta.3.dmg` | `backdown-v1.0.0-beta.3-macos-universal.tar.gz` |
| Windows x86-64 | `BackDown-1.0.0-beta.3-x64.msi` (no administrator rights needed) | `backdown-v1.0.0-beta.3-windows-x86_64.zip` |

The Linux package is called `backdown-io` (since beta.2): Debian and Ubuntu's `backdown` is an unrelated duplicate remover, which ours replaces.

**These builds aren't signed yet.** On macOS, right-click the app and choose Open the first time (for the command-line binary: `xattr -d com.apple.quarantine backdown`). On Windows, SmartScreen may ask you to confirm once. Verify any download against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## What's new since beta.2

- **Folders open in a moment** on very large indexes. On 17 million files and 4 million folders a folder took from half a minute to nearly five minutes to open; now it's a fraction of a second. The background service builds the index that makes this possible once after the upgrade (about a minute there; "Prepare the index for speed" in Activity). Until it's built, folders open as before.
- **The app shows when it's waiting**: anything that takes more than a moment shows a bar along the top of the window, and a note if it's still working after a few seconds.
- **Download names**: the `.deb` files are named with the release's version (`backdown-io_1.0.0-beta.3_amd64.deb`). GitHub had renamed the `~` in earlier names, which broke their download links and their lines in `SHA256SUMS.txt`; both are fixed for beta.1 and beta.2 too.

## Known limitations

- Built locally, not by CI. Every test passed on Linux; the macOS and Windows builds are checked but haven't yet run on those systems. Please report anything that doesn't work: https://github.com/BuenGenio/backdown-releases/issues
- The builds aren't signed or notarised.
- Pro features are free during the beta; pricing may change before 1.0.
