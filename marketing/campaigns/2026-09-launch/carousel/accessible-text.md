# Before you clear that drive

## Slide 1: Before you clear that drive.

Four questions to ask about your archive.

A matching file is a useful finding. A decision about a whole drive needs a fuller picture. Use this checklist alongside your backup plan.

## Slide 2: What has actually been scanned?

01 / Establish the scope

Check which drives and folders are included. Look for failed, skipped, unreadable or unscanned files. A partial inventory cannot account for the files outside it.

## Slide 3: Do the contents match?

02 / Compare the data

Filenames and sizes are clues. Content checks give stronger evidence about the files compared. Review what the comparison covers and what is still unknown.

## Slide 4: Where does the other copy live?

03 / Understand the copies

A repeated folder on the same disk has a different role from a copy on a separate drive. Identify which copies your backup plan needs before removing any.

## Slide 5: Can you open and recover the files?

04 / Test what matters

Try the files you would most need to recover. A hash match is evidence about matching contents; it does not guarantee future recoverability. BackDown’s public alpha helps you inventory and compare.
