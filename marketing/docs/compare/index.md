BackDown isn't a replacement for everything on this list, and it doesn't pretend to be. These pages say plainly what each kind of tool is for, where BackDown overlaps with it, and where it takes a different approach: an index of every drive you own, a hash behind every claim, and nothing deleted without a verified copy elsewhere.
