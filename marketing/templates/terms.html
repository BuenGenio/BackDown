1. Who you're buying from

2. What a pre-order is

A pre-order buys one year of the BackDown edition you choose at checkout: Pro, Team or Forensic (your edition). The year starts on the day your edition is released (the release date), not the day you pay. You receive a signed licence key by email and on the confirmation page as soon as your payment clears; it is checked offline and unlocks your edition's features once they are released.

The paid editions are still in development. The status page shows what exists today and what is planned. The features listed for your edition on the pricing page on the day you pre-order are what you are pre-ordering.

3. Price and tax

You pay the price shown at checkout, in US dollars, once: per machine for Pro and Team, per site for Forensic. Team is sold for 5 machines or more. Sales tax or VAT is calculated from your billing address and shown before you pay. Stripe sends your receipt and invoice by email. The founding-member price applies to the first year; later years are charged at the price in effect then, and only if you choose to renew.

4. Refunds

Before the release date you can cancel and get a full refund at any time, for any reason. After the release date you can still get a full refund within 14 days. If your edition has not been released by {{@ship_by}}, or is released without a feature that was listed for it when you pre-ordered, you can choose a full refund at any time. Refunds go back to the original payment method; a refunded key stops working.

5. Your licence

Your key says how many seats you bought. A Pro or Team seat covers one machine, however many drives or terabytes it looks at. A Forensic seat covers one site: one location of your organisation, however many machines there run BackDown. You can move a seat to a new machine or site. Please don't share your key; it identifies your order.

6. Your data

BackDown runs on your machines and doesn't send your files or file lists to us. What we store about your order is described in the privacy policy.
